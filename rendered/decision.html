0002 · Group duplicate reports by evidenced cause

owner Tomasz Richert proposed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Context

Decision 0001 accepted subject-only merging. #4471 demonstrates that one subject can contain different causes and that reports with different subjects can share a cause. The executable deduplication examples also distinguish form/app channels and tenant form versions. Intake tags are guesses.

Proposed decision

Replace subject equality as the merge criterion with an evidenced shared failure mechanism. Normalize subjects only to find candidates. Read report bodies, resolve tenant locale and booking-form version, and compare channels using the supplied cause rules. Merge only when both causes are known and equal. Leave unknown tenants unplaced for investigation; do not merge merely because their subjects match.

Keep one report per ID, retaining conflicting versions if a future export disagrees. Keep received time separate from merge time. Recalculate counts from evidence and retain anomalous testimony even when the current classifier assigns it to a cause.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading but no longer treats a shared phrase as proof of a shared bug. Separate parser and hint tickets require separate fixes. Preserve decision 0001 unchanged; this new record replaces it only after desk acceptance. Until then this is a proposal, not an accepted policy change.
